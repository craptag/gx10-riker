GX10 Riker privacy policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Who uses this app

GX10 Riker is a private assistant used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public.

What Google data it accesses

With the owner's permission, it accesses the owner's own Google Calendar events: their titles, times, places and notes. It uses this access to show the owner their schedule, to add events and reminders the owner asks for, and to change or delete the owner's own events when the owner confirms.

Where the data is kept

Calendar data is processed and stored only on the owner's own private computer at home. It is not sold, shared, or sent to any third party, and it is not used for advertising or to train AI models.

What it never does

  • It never invites other people to events and never sends emails on the owner's behalf.
  • It never shares calendar data with anyone else.

Removing access

The owner can remove this app's access at any time in their Google Account under Security, then "Your connections to third-party apps & services". Removing access stops all calendar access immediately.

Google API Services User Data Policy

GX10 Riker's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions about this app can be sent to the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for this app.
